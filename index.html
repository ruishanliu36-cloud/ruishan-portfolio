About me

Designing from lived experience.

I was born in China and moved to the United States when I was six. Learning to navigate a new language and environment taught me to notice where people feel confused, excluded, or unsupported.

That experience shapes the way I approach design: I want technology to feel understandable, welcoming, and human. I enjoy turning ideas into interactive prototypes, small games, illustrations, and stories.
