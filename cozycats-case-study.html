CozyCats Pet Care

A mobile app concept designed to help cat owners find and book trusted caregivers while they are away.

User flow Wireframes UI design Prototype

Overview

The challenge

Pet owners may feel uncertain about finding someone trustworthy to care for their pets while they are traveling or unavailable.

I designed CozyCats to make the process easier by giving users a clear way to explore caregivers, review profiles, select an available time, and confirm a booking.

My role

What I worked on

  • Defined the app’s main user journey
  • Created low-fidelity wireframes
  • Designed the visual interface in Figma
  • Organized screens into a clickable prototype
  • Improved the booking and confirmation flow

Process

From idea to prototype

01

Identify the need

I focused on the problem of helping pet owners feel more confident when choosing someone to care for their pets.

02

Plan the flow

I organized the experience into browsing caregivers, reviewing profiles, choosing a time, and confirming a booking.

03

Design the screens

I created a soft visual style and used simple layouts so the experience would feel friendly and easy to understand.

04

Build the prototype

I connected the screens in Figma so users could move through the main booking experience.

Design decisions

Why it looks this way

I used warm neutral colors to create a calm and welcoming feeling. Rounded cards and simple navigation help the interface feel approachable rather than overwhelming.

I also kept each step focused on one main task so users would know what to do next without seeing too much information at once.

Reflection

What I learned

This project helped me understand that designing an app is not only about making screens look attractive. The order of the screens, the wording of buttons, and the clarity of each step all affect how comfortable a user feels.

In a future version, I would conduct user testing and add features such as caregiver reviews, saved profiles, messaging, and clearer availability filters.
